## MOTH v0.5.0 — Vault

**Download:** `MOTH_0.5.0_x64-setup.exe` below. Run it; it updates MOTH and keeps all your memories. Before the database is upgraded, MOTH saves a copy of it in its `backups` folder.

**Coming from v0.4.0 or older?** Install this version by hand once (download and run the installer). Older versions can't update themselves. From v0.5.0 on, MOTH updates itself.

MOTH now holds your own notes as well as what Claude remembers. You get a Markdown editor, a Knowledge Library, links between notes, tabs, and a one-time Obsidian importer that never touches your vault.

### MOTH Notes
- **Three views:** writing, preview, or both side by side. Headings, lists, checklists, tables, code, links, images, `#tags` and `[[wikilinks]]`. Shortcuts: Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+K, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+E.
- **Autosave** about a second after you stop typing, with a visible status. If the same note was changed somewhere else, your text is kept and you choose which version stays. Text you hadn't saved when something crashed is offered back as a draft.
- **History:** earlier versions are kept (at most one every 10 minutes of editing, and always before a rename, move or restore). Restoring an old version keeps the newer one in History too.

### Knowledge Library
- **Organising:** folders (nested, rename, move), collections (groups that don't move anything), tags, files, unresolved links and an archive. Notes can only be deleted for good from the archive, and a folder can only be deleted when it's empty.
- **Search** covers both notes and memories.

### Smart Backlinks
- **Supported links:** `[[Note]]`, `[[Note|text]]`, `[[Folder/Note]]`, `[[Note#Heading]]`, `![[image.png]]` and normal Markdown links.
- **Links panel:** shows links pointing in, links pointing out, links that point nowhere, and related memories.
- **No guessing:** when several notes share a name, the link is marked *ambiguous* and listed with the choices. MOTH never picks one for you.
- **Rename-safe:** renaming or moving a note updates the links that point to it.
- **Suggestions:** related notes are suggested, but nothing is linked until you click Link.

### Workspace tabs
- **Tabs:** notes, memories, projects and The Mind open in tabs, in one or two side-by-side panes. Ctrl+click opens something in the second pane, and you can drag tabs between panes.
- **Restored on restart:** your layout comes back after a restart. Tabs for things that no longer exist are dropped, with a notice.
- **The Mind:** notes appear as small rounded squares, and their links are drawn as lines.

### Import an Obsidian vault
- **Read-only:** your vault is never changed. Nothing in it is created, edited, moved, renamed or deleted, and no tracking files are written into it.
- **Preview first:** MOTH scans the vault and shows real numbers before anything is copied: notes, folders, files, size, links, links that point nowhere, notes with the same name, unsupported files, and whether there's enough disk space.
- **Choices:** keep the folder structure, copy attachments, leave out big files or certain file types.
- **Runs in the background:** you can keep working, pause, resume or cancel. If MOTH or your PC stops in the middle, the import continues where it stopped, and nothing is imported twice.
- **Importing the same vault again** skips notes that were already imported.
- **Undo** removes what an import added. It keeps notes you've edited since, notes your other notes link to, and notes you added links to. Files are only removed when no note uses them.
- **Optional:** let MOTH's local AI read some imported notes and suggest memories. The import itself never needs it.

### MOTH Updater
- **Settings → Updates** shows your version, checks for updates and installs them. MOTH also checks quietly about once a day, and you can turn that off.
- **Signed updates only:** every update must be signed with MOTH's key. Anything unsigned or with a wrong signature is refused, and nothing is installed.
- **Safe before installing:** MOTH saves open notes and your tabs, pauses imports (they continue after the restart), lets background work finish and makes a backup of the database.

### Claude
- **Two new tools:** `moth_search_notes` and `moth_read_note` let Claude search and read your notes. They follow the same per-app project permissions as memories.

### What was tested
- **Engine:** 112 automated tests, all passing. They cover the editor's saving rules, links, rename/move, history, folders, collections, the upgrade from a v0.4.0 database (with the backup copy), and the importer: full import with a check that the vault was unchanged, repeated import, crash and resume, pause, cancel, the disk-space check, unsafe files, undo, the pause around updates, and Claude's note tools with permissions.
- **Large vault** (Linux test machine, 2 cores, 8 GB RAM): 50,000 notes in 2,016 folders plus 1,280 files totalling 5 GB.
  - Scan: 5.7 seconds.
  - Import: 2 minutes 42 seconds, with all 125,516 links connected and no errors.
  - Memory used: at most 187 MB.
  - Search afterwards: about 3 ms.
  - The vault was byte-for-byte unchanged afterwards.
- **App in a browser:** the app was clicked through in a real browser: import wizard, editor, autosave, rename, links, two panes and the restored layout.
- **Windows:** the installer is built and started on a clean Windows machine by the build system.

### Not tested yet / known limits
- **Vaults larger than about 5 GB / 50,000 notes** haven't been tested. A 30 GB vault should work (memory stays bounded and the work is done in batches), but that hasn't been measured.
- **Self-updating** has been checked as far as it can be with one release: the update file is signed, its signature is verified with the app's key, and a tampered copy is rejected. The first real update from one installed version to the next will be v0.5.0 → v0.5.1.
- **Not in this version:** autocomplete for `[[links]]` while typing, and "Ask MOTH" questions about your notes.
- **Not imported:** Obsidian plugins, Canvas files, Dataview queries and theme settings are left out. Unsupported files are listed after the import.
