## MOTH v0.3.1 — The Mind 2.0: Living Atmosphere

**Download:** `MOTH_0.3.1_x64-setup.exe` below. Run it; it updates MOTH and keeps all your memories.

### What's new in The Mind
- **A deep, living background** instead of flat grey: near-black with faint lavender light, a soft dark edge and fine grain.
- **Drifting particles:** tiny specks drift very slowly behind the graph and move slightly when you pan or zoom, so the space feels deep.
- **Living memories:** small white, grey and soft-lavender cores. Starred memories, project hubs and very important memories glow a little and breathe gently, and so does the memory you select.
- **Connection lighting:** hover a memory and its connections light up while the rest fades. Select one and soft light flows along its connections.
- **Areas of thought:** each project forms a softly glowing area with its name. Click a name to glide to that project.
- **Smoother camera:** zoom eases toward your mouse pointer, and any drag or scroll takes over from an animation immediately.
- **Only real activity:** new memories, new connections and updates animate only when MOTH really learns or changes something.
- **New setting:** Settings → General → "Living atmosphere in The Mind" turns the drifting and breathing off. They also stop automatically when Motion is Minimal or Windows is set to reduce animations, and whenever MOTH is hidden.
- **Check smoothness:** The Mind → graph settings → "Check smoothness" measures how smoothly The Mind runs on your computer.

### Fixed in 0.3.1
- **Updating while Claude is using MOTH works.** The installer now closes the copies of the MOTH engine that Claude keeps open for its tools. Before, the update stopped with "Error opening file for writing: moth-core.exe". Claude restarts MOTH's tools by itself.
